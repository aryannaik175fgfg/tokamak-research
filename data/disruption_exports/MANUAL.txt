Disruption-py exports for DIII-D and Alcator C-Mod are not public.

Obtain CSV/parquet from your institution (GA / MIT PSFC) with columns including:
  shot, time, time_until_disrupt

Place files here, e.g.:
  data/disruption_exports/d3d_eval.parquet
  data/disruption_exports/cmod_eval.parquet

Then set paths in data/paths.yaml and configs/eval_cross_machine.yaml.
